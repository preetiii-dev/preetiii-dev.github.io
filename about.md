# About Me

Welcome to Visionary Founder! My name is Nikki. I am a student navigating my accounting journey starting from Matric and moving into ACCA FA1. At the same time, I am building my own tech SaaS startup.

### Financial Disclaimer
I am not a licensed financial advisor or a certified public accountant. The content on this blog represents my personal learning journey, broken thoughts, and creative explorations as an accountancy student applying financial frameworks to real-world software businesses. All content is for educational and informational purposes only.

### Why I Started This Blog
As a visionary mind navigating ADHD, I don't learn from boring textbooks. I learn by connecting completely different worlds together. When I study basic bookkeeping accounts or read corporate insight summaries from places like Deloitte, my brain automatically maps those ideas to human stories, creator trends, and the software logic I use to build my own startup. 

This site is my live notebook. I am sharing my raw perspectives so that other indie tech developers, accountancy students, and business founders can learn these critical finance frameworks alongside me without the corporate jargon.
